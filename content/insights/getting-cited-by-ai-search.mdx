More customers now ask an AI assistant who to hire before they search Google. The assistant answers with a short summary and a handful of sources. If your business isn't one of them, you don't get the click, or even the mention.

This is what we change on a site to make it easier for AI engines to understand, trust and cite.

## Say what you do in the first two sentences

AI engines quote clear, factual statements. Every service page should open with a direct answer: what the service is, who it's for, and where you offer it. Save the brand story for further down.

> **Before:** "We're passionate about helping you unlock your growth potential."
>
> **After:** "We set up and manage GoHighLevel CRMs for real estate brokerages in the UK and the Gulf."

## Use structured data

Schema markup tells machines exactly what each page is. At a minimum:

- `Organization` on every page, with your name, logo, contact email and social profiles
- `Service` on each service page
- `FAQPage` wherever you answer common questions
- `Article` on blog posts, with real publish dates
- `BreadcrumbList` so the structure of your site is explicit

## Answer the questions people actually ask

Collect the questions from sales calls, WhatsApp chats and support emails. Answer each in two to four plain sentences on the relevant page. These question-and-answer pairs are what assistants lift into their responses.

## Publish an llms.txt

An `llms.txt` file at the root of your site gives AI tools a short, plain-text summary of who you are, what you offer and which pages matter. It's a small file that removes guesswork. This site has one at [/llms.txt](/llms.txt).

## Be mentioned elsewhere

AI engines weigh what other sources say about you. Keep your Google Business Profile, directory listings and social profiles consistent, and earn mentions in industry publications and partner sites.

## Measure it

Pick ten questions your customers ask. Ask them in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google every month and record who gets cited. It's manual, but it's the most honest measure there is right now.
