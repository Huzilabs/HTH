Most businesses we talk to don't have a lead problem. They have a reply problem. The enquiry arrives, it waits, and by the time someone calls back the customer has booked with whoever answered first.

You don't need to automate everything to fix that. You need four things, built in this order.

## 1. Get every lead into one place

Before anything else, every lead source has to land in the CRM: website forms, Meta and Google lead forms, portals, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and phone calls. If a source doesn't reach the CRM, nothing downstream can help it.

The test is simple. Submit a test lead from every source and check that each one arrives, with its source tagged.

## 2. Reply instantly, every time

The first reply should go out within a minute, day or night. It doesn't need to sell. It needs to confirm you've received the enquiry, set an expectation and give one next step, usually a booking link.

- Use the channel they contacted you on. A WhatsApp enquiry gets a WhatsApp reply.
- Keep it short and personal. One or two sentences, their first name, your name.
- Stop automation the moment they reply, so a person takes over.

## 3. Text back every missed call

Missed calls are the most expensive leads to lose, because the person was ready to talk. A missed-call text back sends a message automatically when you can't pick up, so the conversation carries on while you're busy.

## 4. Follow up until they answer

Most leads that don't book on the first touch are never contacted again. A short sequence across five to seven days, mixing WhatsApp, SMS and email, recovers a meaningful share of them. Stop it as soon as they reply or book.

## What to leave until later

AI voice agents, complex lead scoring and multi-step nurture are worth building, but only once the four basics above are working. Automating a broken process just makes it break faster.
